AI Assistant
Ask about your rate study
What does this tool do?
It walks a water utility through an 8-step rate study -- customer & billing data (Residential and/or Commercial), meters, rate structure, expenses, non-operating revenue, and water loss -- then calculates the rate structure needed to cover costs.
Fixed Rate vs. Tier-Based Rate -- what's the difference?
Fixed Rate charges every customer the same volumetric rate per 1,000 gallons on top of a base charge. Tier-Based Rate increases the volumetric rate as a customer's usage crosses into higher consumption tiers.
How is the volumetric rate calculated?
It's solved automatically, not entered by hand: (annual operating expenses + contingency) minus non-operating revenue and base charge revenue, divided by billable water volume.
What is the Base Charge based on?
The total monthly base fee across every meter, weighted by each meter size's AWWA equivalent factor, entered on the Meter Distribution step.
How does Water Loss affect the rate?
The cost of lost water is its share of production times the cost to produce each gallon. Water produced is water sold plus water loss. On the Water Loss step you split that cost between customers and the Supply Corporation: the customer share is added to what customers pay through rates (billed on water sold); the utility share is absorbed.
What's the Assessment Fee?
A percentage fee layered on top of rate revenue (e.g. a regulatory or administrative fee), estimated from the calculated rate revenue.
Why is a tier showing a negative rate warning?
The unit cost increase between tiers is too large for the revenue currently needed. It's just a heads-up -- double-check Customer & Connections, Operating Expenses, and the Consumption Tiers -- and it won't stop you from continuing.
Can I save my progress and come back later?
Yes -- use "Save & Continue Later" to generate a link that restores your session. A plain page refresh without that link starts a brand-new analysis.
