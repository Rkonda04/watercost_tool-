Step Details

1. Customer & Connections

Enter the total number of customers (must be greater than zero), the active customers (can be the same as the total, but cannot exceed it), and the total water sold per month in gallons — this drives every rate and water-loss calculation downstream.

2. Meter Distribution

Add one row per meter size using the approved standard sizes (5/8"×3/4" through 10" Compound). Each size auto-fills its AWWA equivalent factor, which you can override. Meter Equivalents = Count × Factor. The tool totals your meters and equivalents and flags it if the total meter count doesn't match your active customers from Step 1.

3. Cost Model, Rate Structure & Assessment Fees

Choose Fixed Rate (a base charge plus one volumetric rate, in $ per 1,000 gallons, for all customers) or Tier-Based Rate (a table of custom consumption tiers with their own customer counts and water sold — ranges must be ascending and non-overlapping, and only the last tier may be open-ended). Only Tier 1's rate is entered manually; every later tier is priced automatically at the previous tier's rate plus the unit cost difference you set, and each tier's revenue updates live in the table. Either way, finish with a single Assessment Fee (%), applied as a percentage of rate revenue.

4. Operating Expenses

Add annual expense line items under Facilities Operation, Facilities Maintenance, Admin, or Miscellaneous (or a custom category) with any billing frequency. Set a Contingency / Inflation % at the bottom — it's applied to the expense subtotal to produce your Total Annual Operating Expenses.

5. Miscellaneous Revenue

Add any revenue that isn't from water sales — grants, interest income, donations, or custom sources. Zero total revenue here is perfectly fine.

6. Water Loss

Answer Yes/No to whether water loss should be reflected in customer rates. If Yes, the required rate is calculated so customer bills recover the cost of the lost water; if No, the utility absorbs that cost and customers pay only for water sold. When enabled, enter gallons lost and/or the loss percentage for each month — at least one of the two per month. The annual total is the sum of the monthly gallons; the average loss % is the average of the monthly percentages.

7. Summary & Results

Click Recalculate to refresh every metric card, the fixed/tier rate breakdown, assessment fee results, and all four charts (expense breakdown, revenue breakdown, monthly water-loss trend, revenue vs. expenses). Click any chart segment, bar, or point to see its exact value in the panel below the chart. Export the full report to Excel or PDF at any time.
